# Mobile-First UX Specification

## Goals

One obvious next action, visible map, one-handed planning, advanced editing outside initial decision, low-distraction ride surfaces, and actionable warnings.

## Navigation

- Plan
- Ride
- Library
- Settings

Record and Free Ride live under Ride.

## Planning flow

### Search
Current location, ride intent, destination/loop, three quick chips, recent/saved places, advanced entry. No full editors, directions, ratings, sharing, or offline packages.

### Choose
Two or three cards with time, distance, surface, one reason, key warning, and provider/confidence where material. Sticky Start Ride; secondary Edit and Prepare. Directions closed by default.

### Edit
Full-screen map editor with points, character, bike, avoid highways/areas, road requirements, undo/redo, and replan.

### Prepare
Single readiness summary: GPS, offline shell, route pack, regional routing, map data, weather, fuel range, warnings, export/share.

## Ride home

Resume active ride, Start Free Ride, Start recording, recent rides, and prepared rides.

## Destructive confirmations

Required for discarding recording, exiting Free Ride unsaved, replacing active ride, deleting settings/regions, clearing requirements, and clearing learning.

## Accessibility

44 px targets, dynamic type, no color-only meaning, logical focus, focus-trapped dialogs, restrained announcements, and usable narrow landscape.

## Copy

Prefer “Mapped gravel,” “Data unavailable,” “Experimental suggestion,” “Approximate match,” and “Offline routing not ready.”

Avoid unsupported “Safe,” “Verified,” “Exact,” “Neural,” “Premium,” and “Live.”
