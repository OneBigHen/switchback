Scoring notes:
- DMD² scores high on ADV/off-road, offline, GPX — it is a cockpit product with offline-first routing.
- GoraAdv scores highest on mixed-surface intent — its T1–T5 hierarchy is unique.
- Calimoto leads curvy-road routing but fails on surface intelligence and closure freshness.
- Scenic leads iOS ergonomics and offline navigation UX.
- Garmin Zumo leads hardware integration, navigation UX, and reliability.
- Trails Offroad and onX lead trail/off-road discovery and land-access intelligence.
